## 6. Founders

Founders is for people who already have something running and a team. Entry is by application. We review every application at our discretion and may approve it, decline it or ask for more context; we are not required to explain a decision.

The membership is annual. The price depends on the cohort you are approved into and stays fixed for you while your membership remains active: opening a new cohort at a different price does not change yours. If your membership lapses and you come back later, the price of the cohort open at that time applies.

The membership gives access to Founders events and spaces and to benefits marked for Founders. It is personal: it is not shared with your team and cannot be transferred.

What is shared in Founders spaces (numbers, problems, plans) is confidential. Do not publish it or share it outside without the permission of whoever said it. Breaking that trust is grounds for removal.
